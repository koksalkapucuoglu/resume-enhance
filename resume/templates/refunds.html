If Pro is not what you needed, ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase and you get the whole amount back. No questions asked, whether or not you used it.

Pro is a one-time purchase that does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel. After 14 days we do not refund the unused part of a period, except where the law requires it or if we shut the service down (see the Terms of Service).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

ResuStack is a resume builder operated by Köksal Kapucuoğlu, who is responsible for your data (the data controller). This page explains what we collect when you use resustackapp.com and the ResuStack MCP server, why, who processes it on our behalf, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. Questions go to privacy@resustackapp.com.

The short version

  • We keep what you put into ResuStack so the product can work: your account and your resumes.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • AI features send your resume text to OpenAI to generate results. A few other service providers, listed below, help run the service.
  • Your password is never stored in readable form.
  • You can delete any resume, or your whole account, yourself.

What we collect

Your account

  • Username and email address, used to sign you in and to reach you about your account.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted one-way hash. We cannot read it, and nobody at ResuStack can see it.
  • Settings and usage: interface language and mode, your plan, and monthly usage counters used to apply plan limits.
  • API tokens you create to connect an MCP client. You can replace or revoke them on your Profile page at any time.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account ID, name and email address with us. We do not store Google access tokens or ask for access to anything else in your Google account.

What you put into ResuStack

  • Resume content: the details you enter or import — typically your name, contact details, links, work history, education, skills, projects and the optional "What I'm working on" section.
  • Earlier versions of a resume, kept as restore points so changes can be undone.
  • Imported files: when you import a PDF or a LinkedIn export, we read the file to extract its text. The file itself is not stored; the extracted details become your resume.
  • Job applications: postings you add (title, company, link and description), match results, and a copy of the resume you attached to each application.
  • Feedback you send us through the site.

Technical data

  • Server logs record technical information about requests and errors, which can include your IP address. We use them only to keep the service running and secure.
  • Short-lived counters tied to your account, used to rate-limit requests.

What stays in your browser

In Agentic Mode, your chat history, the progress of a guided build and which resume you last had open are saved in your browser's local storage, not on our servers. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them. The messages you send are still processed by our server and our AI provider to produce a reply.

Cookies

We use only cookies needed for the site to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a CSRF cookie that protects forms against forgery, and a short-lived cookie that carries one-off notices, such as "saved", to the next page. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use your data

  • To provide the features you use: editing, AI writing and scoring, rendering PDFs, job matching, application tracking and the MCP server.
  • To apply plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • To send account emails, such as confirming your email address and password resets.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

Who processes your data

We share data only with service providers that help us run ResuStack, and only what they need:

  • OpenAI (United States) — when you use AI features, the relevant text is sent to OpenAI to generate a result: resume content, text extracted from imported files, your chat messages and job posting descriptions. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Hetzner (Germany) — hosts the application and stores the database.
  • Cloudflare — provides our domain's DNS and sits in front of the site as a proxy. Traffic to ResuStack passes through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the site.
  • Google — lets you sign in with your Google account if you choose to, delivers account emails through Gmail, and serves the fonts our pages load (Google Fonts).
  • Content delivery networks — our pages load scripts from cdn.tailwindcss.com and unpkg.com. Like Google Fonts, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads those files.

MCP clients you connect. If you connect Claude or another MCP client with your token, that client can read and change your resumes as you instruct it. What that client does with the data is covered by its own provider's privacy policy.

All of these providers are outside Türkiye. For the transfer of personal data abroad, we rely on the explicit consent you give when you create an account. You can withdraw it at any time; because the service cannot run without these providers, withdrawing it means deleting your account from your Profile page.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Resumes, versions and applications are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Deleting a resume also deletes its restore points and the language versions created from it. An application keeps its copy of the resume you sent until you delete the application.
  • Deleting your account from your Profile page permanently deletes your account, settings, resumes, restore points, language versions, applications and API tokens. Feedback you sent is kept without your name attached. This cannot be undone.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Every request to your data is checked against your account, and API tokens can be revoked at any time. No method of storing or transmitting data is perfectly secure, but we work to protect yours.

Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and delete your resumes in the app, and delete your account from your Profile page. For anything else about your data, email privacy@resustackapp.com. Depending on where you live, data-protection law may give you further rights, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority. If you are in Türkiye, see the Turkish version of this policy, which sets out your rights under the KVKK.

Children

ResuStack is not intended for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

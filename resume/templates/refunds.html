Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ terms_updated }}

Pro is a one-time purchase of a fixed period. It starts the moment you buy, and what it adds to the free plan is higher limits. So whether a refund is due depends on whether those extra limits were used.

Full refund within 14 days, if Pro was not used beyond the free plan

Ask within 14 days of your purchase and you get the whole amount back, as long as, since buying, you have stayed within what the free plan allows:

  • no more than {{ limits.ai_credits }} AI credits (imports, AI rewrites, improvement drafts, chat messages), and
  • no more than {{ limits.download_count }} PDF downloads.

In other words: if you bought Pro and found it was not what you needed before relying on it, you are refunded in full.

When a refund is not given

If you have used Pro beyond those limits, the purchase is not refunded, because the service you paid for has been supplied. By completing the purchase you agree that Pro starts immediately, and you acknowledge that this ends the right of withdrawal for digital services where the law allows it.

Always refunded

  • A technical problem on our side that stops you using Pro and that we cannot fix within a reasonable time.
  • A purchase made twice by mistake.
  • Any case where the law where you live requires a refund. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

How to ask

  • Reply to the receipt email from Paddle, or use the link in it to manage your order — Paddle, our merchant of record, processes refunds; or
  • email {{ contact_email }} with the email address you paid with.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show it.

What happens to your account

Pro access from a refunded purchase ends when the refund is made. Your resumes, versions and scores stay; the free plan's limits apply again.

After 14 days

Pro does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel. After 14 days we do not refund the unused part of a period, except where the law requires it or if we shut the service down (see the Terms of Service).

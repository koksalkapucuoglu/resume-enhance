Fonts are always named explicitly. Left to the renderer's default, WeasyPrint and the browser pick different families and the download stops matching the preview. {% endcomment %} {% comment %}Autoescape off: every value below comes from the design catalogue in resume/resume_templates.py, never from a resume.
